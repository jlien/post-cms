This site is a set of static files. We do not run analytics, advertising, or
tracking scripts, and we do not set cookies.

- **What we collect:** nothing automatically. If you email us, we receive your
  email address and whatever you choose to send.
- **What we do with it:** reply to you. We do not sell or share it.
- **Third parties:** the site may be served through a CDN (such as Cloudflare),
  which processes standard request logs under its own privacy policy.
